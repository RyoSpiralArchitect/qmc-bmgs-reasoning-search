## Receipt and failure ceiling

A successful receipt reports fresh metadata inspection plus actual environment
and 32-trace baseline reproduction only. Both PUBLIC archives have zero numerical
traces replayed here; each archive's 64 definitions share one source multiset.
The sealed study stays 64 accepted multisets, 1,024 cells, 512 budget pairs and
256 four-arm blocks, with the original seal, schedule and generation qualifier.
Negative historical results remain unchanged.

There is no detached receipt-admission API. Pure comparators and static/synthetic
tests establish no provenance; no external receipt can skip the actual operation.
Current receiver admission, complete D2 reconstruction, certificate/candidate
issuance, new coordinator qualification, PUBLIC attempts, replication consent,
locked-128 authorization and scientific/performance claims remain false/unmet.

Failures preserve stage, known consumed/unknown state and evidence. Before the
environment call baseline work is zero; during an interrupted environment call
its completed work is unknown, not falsely zero. After a successful full
environment comparison 32 completed baseline traces remain recorded even if
later source/location/output delivery fails. No automatic retry or cleanup.
