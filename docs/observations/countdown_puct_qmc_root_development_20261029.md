# Root-only PUCT-QMC: small positive difference, below the practical threshold

Study date: 2026-10-29. Evidence cutoff: separate analysis completed
13:33:51 UTC; saved-event verification completed 13:55:25 UTC.
Prepared locally for Ryō by the same research agent. This is not independent

## Result

In the registered 512-source comparison, root-only bounded QMC exploration
succeeded in **1,164/2,048 rows (56.8359375%)**, IID in **1,158/2,048
(56.54296875%)**, and fixed PUCT in **286/512 (55.859375%)**.
The sole primary is each source's four-root QMC success average minus its
one deterministic PUCT outcome: **5/512 = +0.9765625 percentage points**.

The unchanged approximate model interval is **[+0.3643933390, +1.5887316610]
percentage points**; half-width is 0.6121691610 points. Although this fixed
model interval is positive, its upper end is below the registered +2-point
practical threshold. The precision-first readout is
**NO_PRACTICAL_UPLIFT_SIGNAL**. This does not establish zero effect, equivalence,
population coverage or general QMC failure. Winner and scientific_decision
remain null.

All 4,608 cells were regenerated byte-identically in the one approved separate
analysis. The subsequent separately implemented, stdlib-only saved-event check
matched every cell reduction, 2,048 QMC/IID pairs and 512 source anchors,
including root-only gating and fixed deep-node scores. It was not a new
experiment, generative replay or independent reviewer. The later result-package qualification is summarized below.

Public evidence: [frozen plan](../preregistrations/countdown_search_value_puct_qmc_root_20261029.plan.json), [source-level outcomes](../results/puct_qmc_series/outcomes.csv), [local verification summary](../results/README.md#verification--検証).

## Fixed policy, branch provenance and units

Candidate `bc405e6249434a16c53e716fb68b516fce0a2902`; plan digest
`ecb21dfb8de2067c2783371d9747bec37f4a7d69e65556eae90eb5c5d1170b0b`.
The existing greedy-rollout-error prior is an **ordinal-rank heuristic**,
not an LM prior or numerical error-gap weight. Every arm retains binary
terminal backup (exact 1, otherwise 0), c=1 PUCT, visits, arithmetic mean/m2,
DAG sharing, proposal cache, canonical ties, transaction budgets and stopping.

Writing U(a)=p(a)*sqrt(1+sum visits)/(1+edge visits), control scores Q+U.
QMC/IID use exactly control for the entire first trajectory. Thereafter only
depth 0 scores Q+U*(0.5+original uniform); depths 1..4 score Q+U.
The fixed gate is trajectory_index>0 and depth==0. Inactive selections do not
plan, generate, charge, consume or advance random material. Actual committed
root draws are indexed contiguously from zero. The instance-local transport
is restored in finally; no old source/global policy was modified.

Deep PUCT acts on the hybrid's current history, not the baseline's history.
Q is not multiplied; m2 and normals are not selector terms. The fixed multiplier
lies in 0.5..1.5, centered at 1, without outcome-based coefficient selection.
Active normals are still generated, saved and coordinate-charged by the
inherited backend. QMC is `torch-sobol-full-sha256-cp-rotation-float51/v2`;
IID is `sha256-counter-open-unit-float51/v2`. This is not Owen scrambling,
Gumbel selection or Bayesian uncertainty. Matching root labels do not imply
common draws or adaptive-QMC convergence.

Branch② was selected as a mechanism-isolation hypothesis from the previous
all-depth hybrid's posthoc audit of all 22 gains/14 losses (27 sources).
Root-only passive flips on 16 successful final trajectories did not imply
their earlier deep histories were identical, and did not predict that these
successes would survive this intervention. The earlier result remains
+0.390625 points / NO_PRACTICAL_UPLIFT_SIGNAL. Its cohort is excluded here;
the two studies are not a paired all-depth-versus-root-only comparison.
The external branch-selection record and all old observations remain fixed.

The new cohort is the first 512 eligible unique solvable sources: six inputs
1..10, target 100..999, generator 26102901, maximum 10,000 attempts. The plan
excludes 2,011 task identities and 1,953 source identities, including the
previous hybrid's 512 sources. Roots are 1048576..1048579 with existing branch-0
derivation. There are 2,048 QMC + 2,048 IID + 512 PUCT physical rows.
PUCT root 0 is a deterministic identity, not four random replicates.

Each row has legal_action_scores cap 512 and six other guards of 100,000.
Total primary cap is 2,359,296. The analysis unit is **512 sources**, not
4,608 cells or 2,048 independent pairs. QMC-minus-IID and IID-minus-PUCT
are descriptive secondary contrasts and cannot replace the sole primary.
